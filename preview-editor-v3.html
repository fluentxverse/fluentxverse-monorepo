1Introduce
Lesson Goal
Can introduce yourself and your coworker.
自己紹介と同僚の紹介ができるようになる。
Situation and Task
You have a new coworker. Introduce yourself and your coworker to this person using the patterns below.
新しい同僚がいます。以下のパターンを使って自己紹介と同僚の紹介をしましょう。
2Present
Useful Patterns よく使うパターン
1My name is John.私の名前はジョンです。
2This is Andrew.こちらが、アンドリューです。
3We are coworkers.私たちは同僚です。
Teaching Notes
Script
"Let's start with the lesson goal. Please read the goal for today."
Instruction

Script
"Let's look at patterns used in introducing yourself and your coworker."
Instruction
Read the first pattern. Ask the student to repeat.
Tip
For lower-level students, give simple definitions.

3 Step 3 — Dialogue
Read the dialogue with your tutor.
講師と一緒に会話を読みましょう。
Tutor:
Good morning. My name is Evan. It's my first day today.
おはようございます。私の名前はエヴァンです。今日は私にとって初出勤の日です。
Student:
Pleased to meet you, Evan. My name is Akihiro. This is Sandra. We are coworkers.
お会いできてうれしいです、エヴァンさん。私の名前は明宏です。こちらはサンドラさんです。私たちは同僚です。
Tutor:
Pleased to meet you, too, Akihiro and Sandra.
明宏さん、サンドラさん、こちらこそお会いできてうれしいです。
4 Step 4 — Complete the Dialogue
Complete the dialogue.
会話文を完成させましょう。
Tutor:
Hello. My name is Shirley. I'm the new secretary.
こんにちは。私の名前はシャーリーです。私は新しい秘書です。
Student:
Pleased to meet you, Shirley. ________________. ________________. ________________.
シャーリーさん、お会いできてうれしいです。________________.
Tutor:
Pleased to meet you, too!
こちらこそ、お会いできてうれしいです。

Guide Questions
1. Hi! Nice to meet you. May I know the names of the others, as well?
2. Do you work with all of them?
3. Are we teammates?
4. I see. Do we often have meetings together?
5. What other things do we do together?

5Challenge — Discussion
Choose one category, then answer the questions.
カテゴリーを一つ選び、質問に答えましょう。
YOUR BOSS
1. What is the name of your boss?
2. What is his/her position in the company?
3. Is he/she a good boss? Why?
YOUR TEAMMATE
1. Can you name one of your teammates?
2. Can you describe him/her?
3. What is his/her task in the team?
YOUR FAVORITE COWORKER
1. Who is your favorite coworker?
2. What do you like about him/her?
3. What is his/her job?
Teaching Notes
Script
"Now, let's have a discussion."
Script
"Please choose a category you would like to discuss."
Instruction
Ask the questions under the student's preferred category.
Tip
If the student can't choose, pick a category. If remaining time, let the student choose another.
