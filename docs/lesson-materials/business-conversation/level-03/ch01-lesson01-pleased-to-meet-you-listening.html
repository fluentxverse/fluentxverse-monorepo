Tutor Guide — Go Live
"Are you ready for the challenge?"
Make the discussion as natural as possible.
Start: "Hello! My name's (your name). What's your name?"
Guide questions: 1) May I know the names of the others? 2) Do you work with all of them? 3) Are we teammates?
End: "Nice to meet you all!"
Give feedback after finishing.
Important: This must be a real-life simulation, distinct from Practice. Corrections should only be done after the exercise.
Step 3
Read the Dialogue
講師と一緒に会話を読みましょう。
Tutor
Good morning. My name is Evan. It's my first day today.
Student
Pleased to meet you, Evan. My name is Minho. This is Sandra. We are coworkers.
Tutor
Pleased to meet you, too, Minho and Sandra.
