# Original Sample: Choosing a Meeting Room

Draft for review, 2026-10-27. Business English Level 3, guided speaking.
Text only; no images. Not assigned to or saved over an existing lesson.
All names, room details, tasks, and dialogue below are original.

## Lesson Objective

I can ask about a meeting room and agree on a room and time that meet our needs.

회의실에 대해 묻고 필요한 조건에 맞는 회의실과 시간을 정할 수 있다.

## Warm-Up - 2 Minutes

You and a coworker need a room for a short meeting. Some rooms are already booked. Check the room information and choose a suitable room together.

동료와 짧은 회의를 할 회의실이 필요합니다. 일부 회의실은 이미 예약되어 있습니다. 회의실 정보를 확인하고 함께 적절한 회의실을 고르세요.

Tutor guide:

1. SCRIPT: "Today's lesson objective is to ask about a meeting room and agree on a suitable room and time. Is it clear?"
2. INSTRUCTION: Give the learner time to respond before introducing the situation.
3. SCRIPT: "We need a room for a short meeting. Some rooms are booked. What do you usually need in a meeting room?"
4. INSTRUCTION: Accept a brief answer and introduce the key language.

## Key Language - 5 Minutes

### Vocabulary

| Word or phrase | Korean | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| meeting room | 회의실 | a room used for meetings | We need a meeting room. |
| free | 비어 있는, 사용 가능한 | available to use | The room is free now. |
| booked | 예약된 | reserved for someone | This room is booked. |
| seats | 좌석 수 | places for people to sit | The room has six seats. |
| screen | 화면, 스크린 | a display for showing information | We need a screen. |
| quiet | 조용한 | with little noise | This room is quiet. |

### Three Patterns

1. **We need a room for [number] people.**
   [숫자]명이 사용할 회의실이 필요해요.
2. **Is [room] free at [time]?**
   [회의실]은 [시간]에 비어 있나요?
3. **Let's use [room] at [time].**
   [시간]에 [회의실]을 사용해요.

Pronunciation focus: stress the room name and time in the question. Model "Is OAK free at TEN?" and have the learner repeat once with a different room and time.

Tutor guide:

1. SCRIPT: "Let's learn the words we need to choose a room."
2. INSTRUCTION: Read each vocabulary item and its example; ask the learner to repeat.
3. INSTRUCTION: Model Pattern 1, then ask for repetition.
4. SCRIPT: "Use this to say how many people need the room."
5. INSTRUCTION: Model Pattern 2, then ask for repetition.
6. SCRIPT: "Use this to check the room and time."
7. INSTRUCTION: Model Pattern 3, then ask for repetition.
8. SCRIPT: "Use this to suggest your choice."
9. INSTRUCTION: Practice the pronunciation focus; correct one important problem if needed.
10. SCRIPT: "Are the patterns clear? Let's check the room information."

## Comprehension - 4 Minutes

Read the information. All rooms are quiet. Each time slot is 30 minutes.

정보를 읽으세요. 모든 회의실은 조용합니다. 각 예약 시간은 30분입니다.

| Room | Seats | Screen | 10:00-10:30 | 10:30-11:00 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oak | 4 | Yes | Free | Booked |
| Cedar | 6 | No | Free | Free |
| Maple | 6 | Yes | Booked | Free |

Read the model with your tutor.

강사와 예시 대화를 읽으세요.

**Tutor:** We have a meeting at ten. Can you help me choose a room?

**Student:** We need a room for three people. Is Oak free at ten?

**Tutor:** Yes. It has four seats and a screen.

**Student:** Let's use Oak at ten.

**Tutor:** Okay. Oak at ten.

Answer the questions.

질문에 답하세요.

1. How many seats does Oak have?
2. Which room has no screen?
3. Is Maple free at ten?

Tutor-only answers: four; Cedar; no, it is booked.

Tutor guide:

1. SCRIPT: "Read the room information first. Then we'll read a short conversation. I'll start."
2. INSTRUCTION: Read the tutor lines; let the learner read the student lines.
3. INSTRUCTION: Ask the three comprehension questions. Check answers against the table.
4. INSTRUCTION: Resolve misunderstandings before starting the drills.

## Drill - 6 Minutes

### Step A: Choose the Correct Word

Choose the correct word in parentheses. Read the complete sentence.

괄호 안에서 알맞은 단어를 고르고 문장 전체를 읽으세요.

1. We need a room (for / at) four people.
2. Is Cedar free (for / at) ten?
3. Let's (use / uses) Maple at ten thirty.
4. Oak (have / has) four seats.

Tutor-only answers: for; at; use; has.

Tutor guide:

1. SCRIPT: "Choose one word in each pair. Please read the whole sentence. Is it clear?"
2. INSTRUCTION: Take the items one at a time. Record the first response before correcting it.
3. INSTRUCTION: If incorrect, model the sentence and have the learner try again. Keep the supported retry separate from the first attempt.

### Step B: Use Cues, Then Choose

Use the room table. Your tutor starts each conversation.

회의실 표를 활용하세요. 각 대화는 강사가 먼저 시작합니다.

| Item | Your information | Your task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Three people, 10:00, Oak | Say how many people need the room. Ask whether Oak is free. |
| 2 | Five people, 10:00, no screen needed | Suggest a room and time. |
| 3 | Six people, 10:30, screen needed | Suggest a room and time. |

Tutor guide:

1. SCRIPT: "For each item, use the cues and the table. You can use the patterns, but say your own sentences."
2. SCRIPT, ITEM 1: "Can you check a room for our meeting?"
3. INSTRUCTION: Wait for the learner to state the group size and ask about Oak. Confirm that it is free at ten.
4. SCRIPT, ITEM 2: "We need a room at ten. Which room can we use?"
5. SCRIPT, ITEM 3: "We need a screen at ten thirty. Which room can we use?"
6. INSTRUCTION: Accept equivalent natural responses. Check required details, then correct after each short exchange.

Tutor-only completion checks:

- Item 1: communicates three people and asks about Oak at 10:00.
- Item 2: chooses Cedar at 10:00; Oak is too small and Maple is booked.
- Item 3: chooses Maple at 10:30; Cedar has no screen and Oak is booked and too small.

## Simulation - 6 Minutes

Five people need to meet for 30 minutes. You need a screen. The meeting is planned for 10:00. Use the table to find a suitable room and agree on a time with your coworker.

다섯 명이 30분 동안 회의를 해야 합니다. 스크린이 필요합니다. 회의는 오전 10시로 예정되어 있습니다. 표를 보고 조건에 맞는 회의실을 찾아 동료와 시간을 정하세요.

Your tasks:

1. State how many people need the room.
2. Check the available rooms and explain any problem briefly.
3. Suggest a later time if needed.
4. Suggest a suitable room and confirm the time.

### Tutor-Only Guide

1. SCRIPT: "I'm your coworker. We need a room for our meeting. Please help me choose one. Is the task clear?"
2. INSTRUCTION: Confirm understanding, then open the roleplay.
3. SCRIPT: "Can we meet at ten? Which room should we use?"
4. INSTRUCTION: Let the learner use the table. Do not suggest Maple or change the meeting time for them.
5. INSTRUCTION: If the learner proposes 10:30 or asks whether it is possible, answer: "Yes, ten thirty is fine." If asked generally about a later time, answer: "Yes. We can start up to thirty minutes later."
6. INSTRUCTION: If the learner proposes a room that does not meet the needs, state only the conflicting fact: "We have five people," "We need a screen," or "Maple is booked at ten." Let the learner revise the proposal.
7. INSTRUCTION: If the learner cannot continue, give one support prompt: "Could we meet at a different time?" Record that help was needed.
8. INSTRUCTION: Confirm a suitable proposal and ask the learner to repeat the final room and time.
9. INSTRUCTION: Give corrections after the exchange. Retry once with the same facts if time allows.

Successful outcome: Maple, 10:30-11:00, with the coworker's agreement. A correct decision must account for availability, five seats, and a screen. Accept simple explanations such as "Oak is too small" or "Cedar has no screen."

Assessment: check the stated need, interpretation of the table, suitable choice, and time confirmation. Record whether each was achieved independently, with a prompt, or after a model. Missing observation is not an incorrect answer.
